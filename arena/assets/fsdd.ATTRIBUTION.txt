Spoken-digit audio in assets/fsdd/ is a curated subset of the Free Spoken Digit Dataset (FSDD):
  https://github.com/Jakobovski/free-spoken-digit-dataset
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)

Subset: 3 speakers (jackson, nicolas, theo) x digits 0-9 x 2 indices = 60 recordings, 8 kHz mono 16-bit WAV.
The arena/audio gate concatenates + distorts these to mint challenge clips; derived clips are likewise CC BY-SA 4.0.
